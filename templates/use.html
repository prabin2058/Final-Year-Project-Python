🔐 Admin Guide

Step 1: Login to Admin Panel

Access the administrative dashboard to manage the system.

  • Click on "Admin Login" button on the homepage
  • Enter your admin username and password
  • Click "Login" to access the dashboard
💡 Tip: Default credentials are username: admin, password: admin123

Step 2: Add New Students

Register students in the system for face recognition.

  • On the dashboard, locate the "Add New User" section
  • Fill in student details: Full Name, Roll Number, Email
  • Click "Add New User" button
  • System will automatically capture training images via webcam
  • Allow the system to capture multiple angles for better accuracy
💡 Tip: Ensure good lighting and the student faces the camera directly

Step 3: Take Attendance

Mark attendance using face recognition technology.

  • Click "Take Attendance" button on the dashboard
  • Webcam will activate and start recognizing faces
  • Position yourself in front of the camera until "CONFIRMED" appears
  • Press 'ESC' key to mark attendance for the last confirmed person
  • Attendance is marked ONLY when you press ESC (prevents false positives)
  • View today's attendance list on the dashboard

Step 4: Manage Users

View and manage registered students.

  • Click "User Details" button to view all registered students
  • See student information: Name, Roll Number, Email
  • Edit or delete student records as needed

Step 5: View Reports & Analytics

Access comprehensive attendance reports and statistics.

  • Click "📊 Reports & Analytics" button
  • View overall attendance statistics and percentages
  • Check individual student attendance records
  • Identify defaulters (students below 75% attendance)
  • Export reports to Excel or CSV format

Step 6: System Settings

Manage system configuration and security.

  • Click "⚙️ Settings" button at the bottom
  • Change admin password for security
  • Retrain face recognition models when needed
  • View system statistics and health

🎓 Student Guide

Step 1: Get Registered

Your admin must first register you in the system.

  • Provide your Full Name, Roll Number, and Email to the admin
  • Admin will capture your face images for training
  • Once registered, you can login to the student portal

Step 2: Login to Student Portal

Access your personal attendance dashboard.

  • Click "Student Login" button on the homepage
  • Enter your Roll Number as username
  • Enter your password (default: your roll number)
  • Click "Login" to access your dashboard
💡 Example: If your roll number is 36, use Username: 36, Password: 36

Step 3: View Your Dashboard

Monitor your attendance and performance.

  • See your overall attendance percentage
  • Check total days present and absent
  • View complete attendance history with dates
  • Get alerts if attendance falls below 75%

Step 4: Mark Attendance (Face Recognition)

Quick attendance marking using face recognition.

  • Click "Let's Attendance" button on homepage
  • Stand in front of the camera
  • System will automatically recognize and mark your attendance
  • Press 'ESC' when done
💡 Tip: Ensure good lighting and face the camera directly for best results
